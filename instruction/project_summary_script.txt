CABADBARAN CITY GARBAGE COLLECTION TRACKING SYSTEM
ROLES AND FUNCTIONS (based on the UI mockups)


SCENARIO
- The Public Viewer (the resident/owner of the household) can view the system to check the current location of the Brgy. Garbage Collector. Once the Brgy. Garbage Collector (tricycle) collects the garbages from the households, it will then be received in the Barangay MRF, and the Barangay will wait for the schedule of the City Hall Garbage Collector (truck) because they will be the one to collect the garbage there and deliver it to the landfill. (Flow: Public Household -> Brgy. Tricycle Collector -> Barangay MRF -> City Hall Truck Collector -> Landfill/Final Disposal)

GENERAL
- each barangay has 4 Tricycle Collectors
- the barangay collector may not collect the garbage for reasons like the garbage is not segregated properly, etc.
- TRK-01 is a sample City Hall Truck Collector number and TRI-01 is a sample Brgy. Tricycle Collector number (the codes used in the mockups)
- there are 31 barangays and each barangay has its own MRF (Materials Recovery Facility), so 31 MRFs in total
- the statuses used throughout the system: Collected, Pending (no status yet), and Not Collected (with a reason)


PUBLIC VIEWER
- a UI/UX display for live map, no account/login needed (open access for the residents)
- can view the map as to where the Brgy. Tricycle Collector and the City Hall Truck currently is (live map with filter for All, Tricycles only, Trucks only, and per barangay)
- can send feedback like if their Garbage was collected or not Collected via anonymous only -- this is through the "Note" card in the Today's Waste page where the public viewer can comment that their garbage wasn't collected, which is the report that shows as the "Not Collected" status in the Brgy. Admin and City Hall Admin monitoring
- a schedule of what type of garbage will be collected in a day of the week (calendar view of the month plus the list per day, e.g. 27 - Biodegradable/Residual Waste, 28 - Recyclable Waste, coming from the Waste Schedule set by the City Hall Admin)
- a "Today's Waste" display showing the current date and the waste type to be collected that day with details/examples (e.g. BIODEGRADABLE -- Yard Waste, Kitchen Waste; NET RESIDUAL -- Diaper, Used Tissue, Sanitary Napkins)


BRGY. ADMIN
- can login
- has dashboard where things can be viewed properly
- dashboard list
1. Main (Pages: Dashboard)
2. Management (Pages: Property List)
3. Monitoring (Pages: Waste Schedule, Live Tracking, Collections)
4. Settings (Pages: History, Profile)
5. Logout Button

- DASHBOARD: Has 4 cards. 1st is the live map where the tricycle collectors of that specific barangay can be monitored. 2nd is the notifications card where all the notifications about the schedule of the City Hall Garbage Collector (the truck that will collect their collected garbages from the MRF) are displayed, e.g. "2 hours from now the garbage collector TRK-01 from City Hall will arrive." 3rd is the collection status data -- e.g. the brgy. garbage collectors are supposed to collect the garbages from 100 households, so it shows the current number of Collected, Pending (meaning no status yet), and Not Collected (meaning the public viewer set the status using the app that no collection happened or the garbage collector didn't collect their garbage). 4th is the recent collection log displaying the Time, Purok, Tricycle Collector number, Status, Reason (if not collected), and Action with a View button/icon to display all the data of that garbage collection entry.

- PROPERTY LIST: Has 3 cards, 1st is for Total Property (the total number of households/establishments in that barangay). 2nd is a form where the Brgy. Admin can input all the data/new data of a household, like the Name of the owner, Property (Household or Establishment), Barangay, and Purok. 3rd is the record display/table of the inputted household/establishment data sorted from the most recently inputted.

- WASTE SCHEDULE: a display of the waste schedule (Day of the week, Waste Type, and Details/Examples of the waste like Kitchen Waste, Yard Waste, etc.) which comes from the City Hall Admin account. For example, for Monday the waste type to be collected is Biodegradable and Net Residual Waste, for Tuesday it is Residual Waste only, and so on.

- LIVE TRACKING: Has 2 cards, 1st is a live map where the tricycles of that specific barangay can be monitored or displayed as to where they are in the map. 2nd is for the notifications about the City Hall Truck arriving.

- COLLECTIONS: Has 3 cards, 1st is for the status data like Pending, Not Collected, and Collected. 2nd is the Overall Collected Load from all the garbage collectors of that specific brgy., where the type of currently collected waste is automatically computed by sack/kg -- like for example how many sacks of Kitchen Waste, kg of Yard Waste, etc., then has a TOTAL like for example 35 sacks and 5 kg. 3rd is the table for the Brgy. collectors (Name, TRI-Number, then View) -- View displays a modal of the total collection of that specific collector. There should be a date filter for this to select the records of specific dates: by default the current date records are displayed, but the admin has a dropdown or setting where he can view the records of the days that have passed (previous days/months, etc.).

- HISTORY: is the record of the days that have passed, but summary only.


CITY HALL ADMIN
- can login (Username, Password, Remember me checkbox, and Forgot Password link)
- has dashboard where things can be viewed properly
- dashboard list
1. Main (Pages: Dashboard)
2. Management (Pages: User Management, Waste Schedule, Tricycle, Truck)
3. Monitoring (Pages: Live Tracking, Property, MRF, Carry-Over)
4. Reports (Pages: History & Reports)
5. Settings (Pages: Profile)
6. Logout Button

- DASHBOARD: Has 7 cards, 1st for Property Collection where the Collected, Pending, and Not Collected numbers are displayed (e.g. 1,248/1,496 collected, 83% completed) and a "View Household Monitoring" button to redirect to the Property monitoring page. 2nd for MRF Collection where the Collected, Pending, and Missed Pickup numbers are displayed (e.g. 18/31 MRFs, 58% completed -- the Missed Pickup refers to the Pickup Truck that will collect the garbages in the barangay MRFs) and a "View MRF Totals" button to redirect to the MRF page. 3rd is for the currently Active Collectors (e.g. 27 active now: 23 Tricycles and 4 Trucks) and a "View Live Monitoring" button to redirect to the Live Tracking page. 4th is the Live Tracking card where the active collectors can be seen in the map, with options/filters to view All, Tricycles only, or Trucks only, then specific barangays, and a fullscreen icon to enlarge the map. 5th is the "Today's Schedule" card displaying the weekly daily type of collection (Monday to Saturday with the waste type and details/examples per day, plus indicators like Today, Tomorrow, In 2 Days, etc.) and a "View Full Schedule" link. 6th is the Recent Collection Activities table (Time, Barangay, Collector, Type, Status, Reason, and Action with a view icon) with a search bar and filters for All Status and All Types. 7th is the "Today's Waste Type" card, today's collection record per sack/kilo displayed with a chart (e.g. Yard Waste, Food Waste, Net Residual -- Diaper, Napkins, etc.).

- USER MANAGEMENT: Has 2 cards, 1st is the "Create New User Account" form where the City Hall Admin creates the accounts of all users in the system (like Barangay Admins, Tricycle Collectors, and Truck Collectors). The input fields are: Full Name* (required), Role* (required, dropdown to select the role of the account), Assigned Barangay (dropdown of the barangays, for Barangay Admins and Tricycle Collectors), Assigned Vehicle (dropdown, for collector accounts only, like TRK-01 for trucks or TRI-01 for tricycles), Contact Number (optional), Username* (required, must be unique), and Password* and Confirm Password* (required, both must match, with a show/hide eye icon), then a "Create Account" button to save the new account to the database. 2nd is the "User List" card which is the record/table of all created accounts sorted from the most recently created -- the columns are Full Name, Username, Role, Assigned Barangay, Vehicle, Status (Active/Inactive), Date Created (with date and time), and Action. Above the table there is a Search bar (search by name or username) and 3 filter dropdowns: All Roles, All Barangay, and All Status, to easily find a specific user. The Action column has 4 icons: View (eye icon -- displays a modal showing the complete details of that user account), Edit (pencil icon -- to update the user's info like reassigning the barangay or vehicle), Reset Password (lock icon -- to reset/change the password of that user in case they forgot it), and Delete (trash icon -- removes the account, with a confirmation prompt first before deleting).

- WASTE SCHEDULE: Has 1 card, the weekly waste schedule table (Day -- Monday to Sunday, Waste Type, and Details/Examples of the waste type like Kitchen Waste, Yard Waste, etc.). This is where the City Hall Admin sets/manages the daily type of waste to be collected -- this schedule is what is displayed to the Barangay Admins, the collectors, and the Public Viewer.

- TRICYCLE: Has 6 cards, the first 4 are summary cards at the top: 1st for Total Tricycle Collectors (the total number of registered tricycle collectors, e.g. 23), 2nd for Active Assignments (collectors that are currently assigned to a route, e.g. 19), 3rd for Unavailable Requests (collectors that reported/requested as unavailable so they need reassignment, e.g. 2), and 4th for Barangays Covered (the number of barangays that already have tricycle assignments, e.g. 31). 5th is the "Current Tricycle Assignment" card, a table of all tricycle collector assignments -- the columns are Collector Name, Username, Assigned Barangay, Purok Coverage (the puroks that the collector covers, e.g. P3, P4, P5), Assigned Tricycle (the tricycle number, e.g. TRI-01), Availability (Available/Unavailable), Status (Active/Temporary Replacement), and Action. Above the table there is a Search bar and 3 filter dropdowns: All Barangay, All Status, and All Availability. The Action column has 2 icons: View (eye icon -- displays a modal showing the complete assignment details of that collector) and Edit (pencil icon -- to update the assignment like changing the purok coverage or the assigned tricycle). 6th is the "Assign Tricycle Collector" card, a form where the City Hall Admin assigns a collector to a route -- the fields are: Select Collector* (required, dropdown of the tricycle collector accounts created in User Management), Purok Coverage* (required, dropdown to select the purok/s the collector will cover), Assign Tricycle* (required, dropdown of the available tricycle units), Status* (required, radio button to choose Active or Temporary Replacement -- Temporary Replacement is used when an assigned collector becomes unavailable and another collector will temporarily take over their route), and Note (optional, e.g. the reason for the temporary replacement), then a "Save" button to save the assignment which will then appear in the Current Tricycle Assignment table.

- TRUCK: Has 6 cards, the first 4 are summary cards at the top: 1st for Total Trucks (the total number of registered trucks, e.g. 4), 2nd for Active Assignments (truck operators that are currently assigned, e.g. 1), 3rd for Unavailable Requests (truck operators that reported/requested as unavailable so they need reassignment, e.g. 1), and 4th for MRFs Covered (the number of barangay MRFs covered by the truck assignments across the 31 barangays, e.g. 31). 5th is the "Current Truck Assignment" card, a table of all truck operator assignments -- the columns are Truck Operator, Username, Covered Barangay MRF (the list of barangay MRFs that the truck will pick up from, e.g. Barangay A, Barangay B, Barangay C), Assigned Truck (the truck number, e.g. TRK-01), Availability (Available/Unavailable), Status (Active/Temporary Replacement), and Action. Above the table there is a Search bar and 2 filter dropdowns: All Status and All Availability. The Action column has 2 icons: View (eye icon -- displays a modal showing the complete assignment details of that truck operator) and Edit (pencil icon -- to update the assignment like changing the covered barangay MRFs or the assigned truck). 6th is the "Assign Truck Operator" card, a form where the City Hall Admin assigns an operator to a truck route -- the fields are: Select Truck Operator* (required, dropdown of the truck operator accounts created in User Management), Assigned Truck* (required, dropdown of the registered truck units), Assigned Barangay MRFs* (required, dropdown to select the barangay MRFs that the truck will cover/pick up from), Status* (required, radio button to choose Active or Temporary Replacement -- Temporary Replacement is used when an assigned operator becomes unavailable and another operator will temporarily take over the route), and Note (optional, e.g. the reason for the temporary replacement), then a "Save" button to save the assignment which will then appear in the Current Truck Assignment table.

- PROPERTY: (this is the Household Monitoring page that the "View Household Monitoring" button in the Dashboard redirects to) Has 4 cards, the first 3 are summary cards at the top: 1st for Pending Property (the properties with no status yet, e.g. 2,458 across all barangays), 2nd for Collected (e.g. 500 across all barangays), and 3rd for Not Collected (the properties where the public viewer/resident reported that no collection happened, e.g. 716 across all barangays). 4th is the "Property Records" card, the table of all household/establishment records from all the barangays (this data comes from the Property List inputted by the Barangay Admins) -- the columns are Name (the owner of the property), Property (House or Establishment), Barangay, Purok, Assigned Tricycle (the tricycle collector covering that purok, e.g. TRI-01), Status (Collected/Pending/Not Collected), and Action with a View icon (eye -- displays a modal showing the complete details of that property and its collection record). Above the table there are 6 filters: Date (calendar picker, by default the current date records are displayed but the admin can select previous dates), Barangay, Purok, Property Type, Collectors, and Status.

- MRF: Has 7 cards, the first 4 are summary cards: 1st for Pending MRFs (barangay MRFs with no pickup status yet, e.g. 12 across the 31 MRFs), 2nd for Not Collected from MRF (the missed pickups, e.g. 5), 3rd for Collected from MRF (e.g. 10), and 4th for Delivered Final Disposal (the loads already delivered to the landfill/final disposal by the 4 trucks, e.g. 2). 5th is the "Overall Collected Load" card where the type of currently collected waste from all the MRFs is automatically computed by sack/kg, e.g. Kitchen Waste 25 sacks, Yard Waste 5 kg, Diaper/Used Tissue/Sanitary Napkins 10 sacks, then has a TOTAL (e.g. 35 sacks 5 kg). 6th is the "MRF" card, the table of the pickup status of each barangay MRF -- the columns are Barangay, Source Schedule Day (the waste schedule day where the garbage came from, e.g. Monday), Waste Type (e.g. Biodegradable + Residual with the details like Kitchen Waste, Yard Waste, Diaper/Used Tissue/Sanitary Napkins), Assigned Truck (e.g. TRK-01), Status (Pending/Collected from MRF/Not Collected), and Action with a View icon (eye -- displays a modal of the complete pickup details). Above the table there are 4 filters: Date, Barangay, Truck, and Status. 7th is the "Delivered to Landfill" card, the table of the loads that were already delivered to final disposal -- the columns are Assigned Truck, Source Schedule Day, Waste Type, Barangay (the barangays included in that delivery, e.g. Barangay A, Barangay B, Barangay C), and Action with a View icon (eye -- displays a modal of the complete delivery details).

- CARRY-OVER: Has 1 card, the "Carry-Over Records" card which is the table of the missed/not collected MRF pickups that are carried over to be collected on another schedule. The columns are Barangay, Source Schedule Day (the original schedule day of that garbage), Waste Type (with the details), Original Assigned Truck (the truck that was originally assigned to pick it up, e.g. TRK-01), Current Assigned Truck (the newly assigned truck, "-" if not yet reassigned), Status (Pending until the carry-over is collected), and Action which has a View icon (eye -- displays a modal of the complete details) and 2 buttons: "Reassign" (to assign a different truck that will collect the carry-over) and "Reschedule" (to set a new collection date/schedule for it).

- HISTORY & REPORTS: Has 2 cards, 1st is the "History Records" card which is the record of the days that have passed but summary only -- grouped per day (e.g. "Today - Monday, April 27, 2026") then listing the summary entries with their time (e.g. 5:00 PM). 2nd is the "Generate Report" card, a form to generate/export a report -- the fields are Report Type (dropdown to select what kind of report, e.g. Property Collection, MRF Collection, etc.), Starting Date and End Date (calendar date pickers to select the date range covered), then a "Generate" button to produce the report.


TRICYCLE GARBAGE COLLECTOR (Mobile App)
- can login (Username, Password, Remember me checkbox, and Forgot Password link)
- has a mobile interface where the pages are accessed through the hamburger/sidebar menu
- sidebar menu list
1. Property (the main/home page)
2. Unavailable for Duty
3. History
4. Profile
5. Logout Button

- PROPERTY: the main page after logging in, has 2 sections. 1st is "Today's Schedule" displaying 2 cards: the current day and date (e.g. Monday, April 27, 2026) and the waste type to be collected for that day (e.g. Biodegradable & Residual Waste) which comes from the Waste Schedule set by the City Hall Admin. 2nd is the "Barangay __ Property Collection" table, the list of the households/establishments in that collector's assigned barangay and purok coverage -- the columns are Name (the owner of the property), Property (House or Establishment), Purok, and Status (blank/pending by default, then becomes Collected or Not Collected once the collector sets it). To set the status, the collector clicks/taps the name of the owner in the table and it opens the collection entry page for that property.

- COLLECTION ENTRY (COLLECTED): after tapping the name of the owner, the entry page displays the "NAME SELECTED" card showing the owner's name, purok, and the property's note/tag (e.g. None Composting), with a status dropdown where the collector selects "Collect". Below it is a card of the automatically captured data: GPS Location, Timestamp, and the collector's tricycle number (e.g. TRI-01). Then the "Waste Quantity" card, a table where the collector inputs the collected garbage per waste type -- the columns are Waste Type (e.g. Kitchen Waste, Yard Waste, Diapers/Used Tissue/Sanitary Napkins), Unit (dropdown to select Sack or Kilo), and Quantity (dropdown to select the amount, e.g. 2), then a "SAVE" button to submit the entry. The property's status in the table becomes "Collected" and this inputted data is what is automatically computed by sack/kg in the collection totals of the Barangay Admin and City Hall Admin.

- COLLECTION ENTRY (NOT COLLECTED): if ever the garbage can't be collected, after tapping the name of the owner the collector selects "Not Collected" in the status dropdown of the "NAME SELECTED" card (which also shows the owner's name, purok, and note/tag e.g. Has Special Waste, plus the auto-captured GPS Location, Timestamp, and tricycle number). The form displays: "REASON FOR NOT COLLECTED*" (required, dropdown to select the reason), "IMAGE PROOF" (tap to upload an image in JPG/PNG format as the evidence/proof why it was not collected), and "NOTE" (optional text area for additional details), then a "SAVE" button to submit. The property's status becomes "Not Collected" with the reason recorded -- this is the status and reason that will be displayed in the recent collection logs of the Barangay Admin and City Hall Admin.

- UNAVAILABLE FOR DUTY: Has 2 cards, 1st is the collector's information card displaying their own details: Collector Name (e.g. Abayon), Role (Tricycle Collector), Assigned Barangay (e.g. Mabini), Assigned Purok (e.g. Purok 1), and Assigned Tricycle (e.g. TRI-01). 2nd is the "Submit Unavailable Request" form used if ever the collector will not be available to report for duty -- the fields are: Affected Date (calendar date picker, the date the collector will be unavailable), Unavailable Until (optional, calendar date picker, if the collector will be unavailable for multiple days -- if left blank the request is for the affected date only), Reason (input for the reason of the unavailability, e.g. sick, family emergency, etc.), and Notes/Explanation (optional, text area for additional details), then a "Submit Request" button to submit it. Once submitted, the collector's availability becomes Unavailable and the request will be counted/displayed in the "Unavailable Requests" card of the Tricycle page in the City Hall Admin dashboard, so the admin can reassign the route to another collector as a Temporary Replacement.

- HISTORY: the record of the collector's own past collection entries, but per-entry records. It has a Search bar (to find a specific property owner) and a Date filter (current date by default, but the collector can select previous days) at the top, then the "Property Collection History" section where the entries are displayed as cards sorted from the most recent. Each record card displays: the Name of the property owner (e.g. Nica Abayon) with the status badge on the top right (Collected or Not Collected), Property Type (e.g. House), Purok, the schedule batch it belongs to (e.g. Monday Batch), and Waste Type (e.g. Biodegradable + Residual), then on the right side the Location, date (e.g. April 27, 2026), time (e.g. 9:10 AM), and the tricycle number (e.g. TRI-01), and below it the breakdown of the collected waste quantity per type (e.g. Kitchen Waste 15 sacks, Yard Waste 3 kg, Diapers/Used Tissue/Sanitary Napkins 10 sacks).

- PROFILE: Has 2 cards, 1st is the "Profile Information" card displaying the collector's own account details (view only, since these come from the account created by the City Hall Admin in User Management): profile picture, Full Name (e.g. Nica D. Mangubat), Username (e.g. nica@123), Role (Tricycle Garbage Collector), Assigned Barangay (e.g. Mabini), Purok (e.g. 1), Assigned Tricycle (e.g. TRI-01), Contact Number, and Account Status (Active/Inactive). 2nd is the "Change Password" card, a form where the collector can change their own password -- the fields are Current Password, New Password, and Confirm Password, then an "Update Password" button to save the new password and a "Cancel" button to discard the changes.


TRUCK GARBAGE COLLECTOR (Mobile App)
- can login (Username, Password, Remember me checkbox, and Forgot Password link) -- same login design as the Tricycle Collector app
- has a mobile interface where the pages are accessed through the hamburger/sidebar menu
- sidebar menu list
1. MRF (the main/home page)
2. Unavailable for Duty
3. History
4. Profile
5. Logout Button

- MRF: the main page after logging in, has 3 sections. 1st is "Today's Schedule" displaying 2 cards: the current day and date (e.g. Monday, April 27, 2026) and the waste type to be collected for that day (e.g. Biodegradable & Residual Waste) which comes from the Waste Schedule set by the City Hall Admin. 2nd is the "Overall Collected Load" card, the running total of the truck's pickups for the day -- it has a "Total from MRF's Collected" row showing how many MRFs have been picked up so far (e.g. 2 MRF's), then the automatically computed quantity per waste type (e.g. Kitchen Waste 25 sacks, Yard Waste 5 kg, Diapers/Used Tissue/Sanitary Napkins 10 sacks) and a TOTAL row (e.g. 35 sacks 5 kg), then a "Deliver to Landfill" button below it to submit/mark that the whole load was delivered to final disposal -- this is what updates the "Delivered Final Disposal" count and adds the record to the "Delivered to Landfill" table in the City Hall Admin MRF page. 3rd is the "Assigned Barangay MRFs" section, the list of the barangay MRFs assigned to that truck operator displayed as cards -- each card shows the MRF name (e.g. Barangay A MRF) with a status badge on the top right (Collected, or none/pending if not yet picked up), the Source Schedule (e.g. Monday), Waste Type (e.g. Biodegradable + Residual), and the breakdown of that MRF's load per waste type (e.g. Kitchen Waste 15 sacks, Yard Waste 3 kg, Diapers/Used Tissue/Sanitary Napkins 10 sacks) which comes from the collections data of that barangay. Tapping an MRF card opens the pickup entry page for that MRF.

- PICKUP ENTRY (COLLECTED): after tapping a barangay MRF card, the entry page displays the "SELECTED BARANGAY" card showing the barangay name (e.g. Barangay M) with a status dropdown where the operator selects "Collect", then below it the card of the automatically captured data: GPS Location, Timestamp, and the truck number (e.g. TRK-01). Unlike the Tricycle Collector, the truck operator no longer inputs the waste quantities since the load per MRF is already computed from that barangay's collection data -- confirming "Collect" marks that MRF as Collected from MRF (updates the status in the City Hall Admin MRF monitoring) and its load is added to the Overall Collected Load of the truck.

- PICKUP ENTRY (NOT COLLECTED): if ever the MRF pickup can't be done, after tapping the barangay MRF card the operator selects "Not Collected" in the status dropdown of the "SELECTED BARANGAY" card (e.g. Barangay M), with the card of the automatically captured data below it (GPS Location, Timestamp, and the truck number e.g. TRK-01). The form displays: "REASON FOR NOT COLLECTED*" (required, dropdown to select the reason) and "NOTE" (optional, text area for additional details), then a "SAVE" button to submit. The MRF's status becomes Not Collected -- this is what is counted in the "Missed Pickup"/"Not Collected from MRF" cards of the City Hall Admin, and the missed pickup is added to the Carry-Over Records so the admin can Reassign or Reschedule it.

- HISTORY: the record of the truck operator's own past entries, but per-entry records. It has a Search bar and a Date filter at the top (current date by default, but can select previous days), then 2 sections: 1st is the "MRF Pickup History" where each pickup entry is displayed as a card sorted from the most recent -- each card shows the MRF name (e.g. Barangay A MRF) with the status badge on the top right (Collected or Not Collected), Source Schedule (e.g. Monday), Waste Type (e.g. Biodegradable + Residual), the load breakdown per waste type (e.g. Kitchen Waste 15 sacks, Yard Waste 3 kg, Diapers/Used Tissue/Sanitary Napkins 10 sacks), and on the right side the Location, date (April 27, 2026), time (9:10 AM), and truck number (TRK-01). 2nd is the "Final Disposal Delivery History" where each landfill delivery is displayed as a card -- each card shows the truck number (e.g. TRK-01) with a "Delivered to Landfill" badge on the top right, the schedule batch (e.g. Monday Batch), Waste Type, the delivered load breakdown per waste type with a Total row (e.g. Total 25 sacks 3 kg), the list of the MRFs included in that delivery (e.g. MRFs: Barangay M, Barangay B, Barangay C), and on the right side the Location, date, time, and truck number.

- UNAVAILABLE FOR DUTY: Has 2 cards, 1st is the operator's information card displaying their own details: Collector Name (e.g. Abayon), Role (Truck Collector), Assigned Barangay (the list of covered barangay MRFs, e.g. Barangay M, Barangay B, Barangay C), and Assigned Truck (e.g. TRK-01). 2nd is the "Submit Unavailable Request" form used if ever the operator will not be available to report for duty -- the fields are: Affected Date (calendar date picker, the date the operator will be unavailable), Unavailable Until (optional, calendar date picker, if unavailable for multiple days -- if left blank the request is for the affected date only), Reason (input for the reason of the unavailability), and Notes/Explanation (optional, text area for additional details), then a "Submit Request" button to submit it. Once submitted, the operator's availability becomes Unavailable and the request will be counted/displayed in the "Unavailable Requests" card of the Truck page in the City Hall Admin dashboard, so the admin can reassign the route to another operator as a Temporary Replacement.

- PROFILE: Has 2 cards, 1st is the "Profile Information" card displaying the operator's own account details (view only, since these come from the account created by the City Hall Admin in User Management): profile picture, Full Name, Username, Role (Truck Garbage Collector), Assigned Barangay (the list of covered barangay MRFs, e.g. Barangay M, Barangay B, Barangay C), Assigned Truck (e.g. TRK-01), Contact Number, and Account Status (Active/Inactive). 2nd is the "Change Password" card, a form where the operator can change their own password -- the fields are Current Password, New Password, and Confirm Password, then an "Update Password" button to save the new password and a "Cancel" button to discard the changes.
